#### MCP: join tables of many-to-many associations can be queried ([#5016](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5016))

- the owning-side `#[ORM\ManyToMany]` property of every entity marked `#[AsMcpTable(exposed: true)]` must now declare `#[AsMcpTable(exposed: bool)]` (the attribute can be placed on properties now), otherwise the `shopsys.mcpJoinTableExposure` PHPStan error is reported
    - `#[AsMcpColumn]` on such a property is reported by the `shopsys.mcpJoinTableColumnAttribute` PHPStan error, the join table is decided by `#[AsMcpTable]` only
    - `exposed: true` makes the join table with both its join columns queryable via MCP, `exposed: false` keeps it hidden
    - add the attribute to your own many-to-many associations of exposed entities and to the ones you extend or override
