#### Respect Packeta pickup point opening hours in the pickup date ([#5080](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5080))

- the storefront now sends the dates the selected Packeta pickup point is closed on when changing the transport in the cart, so the expected delivery date respects the opening hours of the point
- the `changeTransportInCart()` function returned by `useChangeTransportInCart()` now accepts the whole transport instead of its UUID, so update your custom calls accordingly
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
