#### Articles and blog articles are rendered from content editor documents instead of GrapesJS HTML ([#TBD](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/TBD))

- the GraphQL fields `Article.text` and `BlogArticle.text` were replaced by `content` (JSON), update your queries and fragments (`ArticleDetailFragment`, `BlogArticleDetailFragment`) and run `pnpm run gql` to regenerate the types
- the content is a document `{ version, profile, blocks }` (`TypeContentDocument` in `types/contentEditor.ts`), render it with the new `ContentRenderer` instead of `UserText`: `<ContentRenderer document={content} products={products} areProductsFetching={areProductsFetching} />`
    - products of the `products` block are loaded for the whole document by `useDocumentProducts()` (`utils/contentEditor/useDocumentProducts.ts`)
    - `ContentRenderer` finds the component of every block in the map `blockComponents` (`components/Blocks/ContentEditor/blockComponents.ts`); the components are in `components/Blocks/ContentEditor/blocks/`, add your own blocks to the map, see [Content Editor Blocks](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/extensibility/content-editor-blocks/)
- the following were removed together with GrapesJS, remove their usages from your project
    - `components/Basic/UserText/GrapesJs.tsx`, `GrapesJsParser.tsx` and `GrapesJsProducts.tsx`
    - `utils/parsing/grapesJsParser.ts` and its test
    - `styles/grapesjs.css` and its imports in `styles/globals.css` and `styles/user-text.css`
    - the page `pages/grapesjs-template.tsx` (replaced by `pages/content-editor-preview.tsx`) and the entries `grapesjs-homepage-article-template` and `grapesjs-article-template` in the `matcher` of `middleware.ts`
    - the `isGrapesJs` prop of `UserText`, the component renders only the passed `htmlContent` now
- `styles/user-text.css` no longer contains the rules for GrapesJS markup (`.gjs-*`, `.row`, `.column`, `.grid__item`, `.image-position-*`, `.button-link-position-*`), only the typography, lists, tables and iframes of the user text stay; the blocks style themselves with Tailwind classes
- the administration shows the live preview of the content in the new page `pages/content-editor-preview.tsx`, add `content-editor-preview` to the exclusions of the `matcher` in your `middleware.ts`
    - the page accepts documents only from the origin of the storefront, set the new environment variable `CONTENT_EDITOR_PREVIEW_ORIGINS` (comma-separated origins) when the administration runs on a different host, e.g. `CONTENT_EDITOR_PREVIEW_ORIGINS=https://admin.example.com`
    - the variable is read into the public config as `contentEditorPreviewOrigins` (`buildPublicEnvConfig.ts`, `envConfig.ts`), add it to your mock of the public config in tests
- the tid `grapesjs_product_hero` was renamed to `article_product_hero`, new tids `content_block_text`, `content_block_heading`, `content_block_image`, `content_block_button_link`, `content_block_video`, `content_block_map`, `content_block_iframe`, `content_block_products`, `content_block_columns` and `content_editor_preview_ready` were added to `cypress/tids.ts`
- the demo block `faq` (`FaqBlock.tsx`, `FaqBlockData` and the tid `content_block_faq`) was added to `project-base` as an example of a project block, see [Adding a Content Editor Block](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/cookbook/adding-a-content-editor-block/)
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
