#### Cart price calculation ([#4247](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4247))

- updated cart GraphQL types to include detailed price breakdown fields for better discount visibility
    - `totalItemsPriceBeforeDiscount` - total price before any discounts applied
    - `totalProductDiscountPrice` - total savings from products on sale
    - `totalPromoCodeDiscountPrice` - total savings from promo codes
    - `totalDiscountPrice` - combined total savings
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
