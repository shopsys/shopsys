#### Reorganize the administration menu ([#5072](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5072))

- the administration side menu was restructured, see `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\AdminNavigation\SideMenuBuilder::createMenu()`, update everything that extends or references the menu in your project
    - `SideMenuBuilder` constants `SECTION_LISTS` and `SECTION_STOCKS` were removed together with their sections, the items were moved:
        - flags, parameters, parameter groups, color parameter values (the last three into the new `SECTION_PARAMETERS`), brands and measurement units to `ROOT_PRODUCT`
        - shipping and payment methods, transport groups, stores, warehouses, warehouse settings and closed days to the new `ROOT_TRANSPORT_AND_PAYMENT`
        - order statuses, complaint statuses, countries and phone prefixes directly to `ROOT_SETTING`
    - other items changed their parent:
        - orders (`LIST_ORDER`), inquiries (`LIST_INQUIRY`) and complaints are not root items anymore, they are children of the new `ROOT_SALES`
        - promo codes are in the new `SECTION_PROMO_CODES` in `ROOT_PRICING`, `LIST_PROMO_CODE_BATCH` is a sibling of `LIST_PROMO_CODE` now, not its child
        - gift vouchers were moved to `ROOT_PRICING`, free shipping and payment to `ROOT_TRANSPORT_AND_PAYMENT` and bestsellers to `ROOT_PRODUCT`
        - e-mail settings and templates, newsletter subscribers, the contact form and the order confirmation page were moved to `SECTION_COMMUNICATION`
        - `SECTION_LEGAL`, `SECTION_SEO` and `SECTION_COMMUNICATION` are root sections now, they were children of `ROOT_CMS` and `ROOT_SETTING`
    - the sections with a single item were replaced by the item itself, constants `SECTION_IDENTIFICATION`, `SECTION_CONTACT_FORM`, `SECTION_CONSTANTS`, `HEUREKA_SETTINGS` and `LIST_FILE` were removed, use `LIST_DOMAIN`, `CONTACT_FORM_SETTINGS`, `LIST_CONSTANT`, `SECTION_HEUREKA` and `ROOT_FILE` instead
    - if you place your CRUD controllers with `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Config\CrudConfig::setMenuSection()`, update the sections and the items they are positioned relative to
        - a menu section or a submenu section that no longer exists (e.g. `setMenuSection('settings', 'lists')`) throws a `LogicException` when the menu is built
        - a position relative to an item that is no longer in the section (e.g. `['after' => 'flags']` in `ROOT_SETTING`) is not reported, the item is appended last
    - if you change the menu in a subscriber of `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\AdminNavigation\ConfigureMenuEvent`, check your `getChild()` and `removeChild()` calls against the new structure
        - `getChild()` of a moved item returns `null` and `removeChild()` of a moved item does nothing, so an item you removed is displayed again
        - all existing events are still dispatched for the same items, events `SIDE_MENU_SALES`, `SIDE_MENU_FILES`, `SIDE_MENU_TRANSPORT_AND_PAYMENT`, `SIDE_MENU_LEGAL`, `SIDE_MENU_SEO` and `SIDE_MENU_COMMUNICATION` were added for the root sections that did not have one
    - if you extend `SideMenuBuilder`, compare your overridden `create*Menu()` methods with the new ones, the items created in `createOrdersMenu()` and `createInquiriesMenu()` no longer have an icon
    - if you override the template `@ShopsysAdministration/partial/side_menu.html.twig`, add `or matcher.isAncestor(item)` to the condition of the class `show` in the block `simple_element`, otherwise a root item without a submenu (e.g. Files) is not highlighted on its subpages
- the page "Shippings and payments" was split into the pages "Shipping methods" and "Payment methods"
    - the route `admin_transportandpayment_list` (`/transport-and-payment/list/`), `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Controller\Admin\TransportAndPaymentController::listAction()`, the template `@ShopsysAdministration/content/transportAndPayment/list.html.twig` and the constant `SideMenuBuilder::LIST_TRANSPORT_AND_PAYMENT` were removed
        - use the routes `admin_transport_list` (`/transport/list/`) and `admin_payment_list` (`/payment/list/`) and the constants `LIST_TRANSPORT` and `LIST_PAYMENT` instead
        - `NEW_TRANSPORT`, `EDIT_TRANSPORT`, `NEW_PAYMENT` and `EDIT_PAYMENT` are children of `LIST_TRANSPORT` and `LIST_PAYMENT`
        - the pinned menu items of the removed page are migrated to both new pages
    - `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Controller\Admin\TransportController::listAction()` and `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Controller\Admin\PaymentController::listAction()` are pages with their own routes now, the templates `@ShopsysAdministration/content/transport/list.html.twig` and `@ShopsysAdministration/content/payment/list.html.twig` extend the administration layout
        - if you override these templates or render the actions with `render(controller(...))`, update them
- the role sections in the form of an administrator role group copy the root sections of the menu
    - `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Security\Role\AdminRoleSectionsProvider` constants `ORDERS_CUSTOMERS`, `PRODUCTS_CATALOG`, `MARKETING_PROMOTIONS`, `CONTENT_MANAGEMENT`, `SETTINGS_CONFIGURATION`, `LEGAL_COMPLIANCE`, `SEO_MARKETING_TOOLS` and `SYSTEM_TOOLS` were replaced with `SALES`, `CUSTOMERS`, `CATALOG`, `PRICING`, `TRANSPORT_AND_PAYMENT`, `CONTENT`, `LEGAL`, `SEO`, `COMMUNICATION`, `INTEGRATIONS`, `ADMINISTRATORS` and `SETTINGS`
        - assign your roles to the new sections, a role with a section that does not exist is displayed in a separate group named "Other"
    - `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Security\Role\CoreAdminRoleProvider` methods `getOrdersAndCustomersRoles()`, `getProductsAndCatalogRoles()`, `getMarketingAndPromotionsRoles()`, `getContentManagementRoles()`, `getSettingsAndConfigurationRoles()`, `getLegalAndComplianceRoles()`, `getSeoAndMarketingToolsRoles()` and `getSystemToolsRoles()` were replaced with `getSalesRoles()`, `getCustomersRoles()`, `getCatalogRoles()`, `getPricingRoles()`, `getTransportAndPaymentRoles()`, `getContentRoles()`, `getLegalRoles()`, `getSeoRoles()`, `getCommunicationRoles()`, `getIntegrationsRoles()`, `getAdministratorsRoles()` and `getSettingsRoles()`
        - if you override them, move your changes to the methods of the new sections
    - the identifiers of the role sections equal the names of the root menu sections, `CrudConfig::setCustomRoleSection()` is needed only for a CRUD controller placed in a nested menu section
    - no data migration is needed, the roles did not change and role groups store only the roles
- the administration calls `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Article\Article` a "page" instead of an "article", the English texts use "shipping" instead of "transport" and several menu items and headings were renamed
    - translation message IDs were renamed (e.g. `New article` to `New page`, `Articles in footer` to `Pages in footer`, `Transport type` to `Shipping type`, `Free transport and payment` to `Free shipping and payment`, `Email newsletter` to `Newsletter subscribers`, `Advertising system` to `Advertising`), blog articles are not affected
        - if you use or translate these messages in your project, run `php phing translations-dump` and translate the new message IDs
    - the Czech translations use "produkt" instead of "zboží" and many Slovak translations were corrected
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
