#### Preserve removed gifts and unify purchase and order presentation ([#4966](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4966))

- if you override `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\GiftPlan\GiftCartFacade::refreshProductGiftsInCart()`, `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\GiftPlan\GiftCartFacade::refreshProductGiftCartItemsAndGetInvalidItems()`, `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Cart\CartFacade::removeItemFromExistingCartByUuid()`, `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Cart\Cart::removeItemById()`, `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Model\Cart\CartWatcherFacade::checkStockQuantities()` or `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Model\Cart\MergeCartFacade::mergeCarts()`, update them accordingly
    - every gift is now a separate cart item of the product item it belongs to (see `CartItem::getRelatedItems()` and `Cart::findParentItem()`), even when the same gift is offered for more products, so `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\GiftPlan\GiftCartItemSetup` is created per pair of the main product and the gift
    - `GiftCartItemSetup` is now immutable and exposes `$mainProduct`, `$giftProduct` and `$quantity` as public readonly properties, so `GiftCartItemSetup::getGiftProduct()`, `GiftCartItemSetup::getQuantity()` and `GiftCartItemSetup::addQuantity()` were removed and its constructor now takes the main product as the first argument and the gift product as the second one
    - a gift removed by the customer is recorded in `CartFacade::removeItemFromExistingCartByUuid()` by `Cart::addRemovedProductGift()`, must not be added back while `Cart::isProductGiftRemoved()` returns true, is forgotten by `Cart::removeItemById()` when its main product leaves the cart, and is carried over during cart merging by `MergeCartFacade::mergeRemovedProductGifts()`
    - gifts are not copied during cart merging, they are recalculated from the merged products
    - `CartWatcherFacade::checkStockQuantities()` skips gift items, their stock is allocated by `GiftCartFacade`
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
