#### storefront: form fields and the product list sorting now share one visual language ([#5047](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5047))

- form fields and buttons are softer and fields have room for their floating label
    - buttons and fields share the `control` radius (`--radius-button` and `--radius-input`, 10px); if you set your own radius tokens, check that buttons and fields still share one shape
    - `TextInput`, `Textarea`, `Select`, `MultiSelect` and `DatePicker` have a 1px border; hover darkens it and a click thickens it to 2px with an inset ring, so the content does not move; an error and a warning thicken it the same way on the fields that show them (`TextInput`, `Textarea`, `MultiSelect`, the phone prefix select); use `getFieldStateClassName()`, `getFieldHeightClassName()` and the `FieldSize` type from `components/Forms/Lib/fieldUtils.ts` for your own fields
    - the default field stays 56px high on every width and its floating label is now 12px, 8px from the top; the small field is 40px (was 48px), as high as a `medium` button; review your custom `h-*` and `pt-*` overrides on fields and labels, and the error icon offsets for the small field in your copy of `FormLineError`
    - a `large` button next to a default field uses `min-h-14` to match its 56px height, so `ApplyCodeForm` and `NewsletterForm` use the default field size; check the overrides on your buttons placed next to a field
    - `TextInput` passes its `className` to the field and to its floating label, so pass only border and text classes there (`NewsletterForm` no longer passes a background, which covered the label on keyboard focus)
    - autofilled fields keep the field background on hover and focus instead of the checkbox fill color (`input-fill`)
    - `SearchInput` uses the same 1px border, hover and focus as the other fields and stays 48px high; remove your `border-*` and `rounded-*` overrides passed in its `className` (e.g. the mobile search overlay and the new complaint page no longer pass them)
    - the options list of `Select` and `MultiSelect` is detached from the field (4px gap, own border and shadow); remove your `rounded-b-*` and `border-t-*` overrides passed in `listClassName` or `selectClassName` of `Select` and in `listClassName` or `inputClassName` of `MultiSelect`
    - `Spinbox` draws the field border as an overlay (`after:border-input-border-default`); hover darkens it and focusing the value thickens it to 2px; its decrease button no longer has the gray fill; check your own spinbox styling
    - `--color-input-border-warning` is now `orange-700` for a 3:1 contrast, and the new `--color-input-bg-disabled` gives disabled fields a gray background; set both if you override the input colors
    - the selected filter chip uses the `--color-tag-*` and `--radius-tag` tokens and stays a pill; check your overrides of these tokens
    - `twMergeCustom()` now knows the radius tokens of the theme, so a later `rounded-*` class overrides `rounded-input`, `rounded-button` and the other tokens; check your className overrides that combine them
    - run `pnpm compile-tailwind-for-admin` to regenerate `public/tailwind-for-admin/style.css`
- keyboard focus fills text fields like buttons and links, a click only thickens the border
    - the new `focus-keyboard` variant in `styles/base.css` matches a focused element only while the customer navigates with Tab; it reads `<html data-focus-source>`, which the new `useFocusSource()` from `utils/accessibility/useFocusSource.ts` keeps up to date
    - the new `focus-within-keyboard` variant does the same for an element that contains the focus; `Select` and `MultiSelect` use it, so the fill also shows when the search input of a combobox is focused
    - call `useFocusSource()` in your `pages/_app.tsx`, otherwise fields never get the keyboard focus fill
    - use `focus-keyboard:` instead of `focus:` for a focus fill on your own text fields
- the product list sorting bar is lighter and fits one row on mobile
    - from 1024px the options are tabs styled like the review sorting (`getSortingTabClassName()` in `components/Blocks/SortingBar/sortingBarUtils.ts` is shared by both); at most 4 options are visible, the rest is under "More" and the selected option always stays visible; in your tests, open "More" (`blocks_sortingbar_more_button`) before clicking an option after the fourth
    - the bar no longer has the full-width bottom border, and the sorting is not rendered when there is only one option; check your custom sort options
    - the desktop options are links in `<nav>` with `aria-current` instead of `role="listbox"` and `option` with `aria-selected`, and the mobile dropdown is a disclosure (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, no `aria-haspopup`) with links instead of `role="menu"` and `menuitem`; update your tests and selectors that rely on these roles
    - `SortingBarItem` and `SortingBarOptions` accept `variant: 'tab' | 'dropdown'` instead of `role` and `itemRole`, `SortingBarItem` requires `href` and `onClick`, and `MobileSortingActions` requires the new `sortOptionsShortLabels`; a click with a modifier key (Ctrl, Cmd, Shift, Alt) now keeps the browser behaviour (e.g. opens a new tab), and the option links lead to the same URL as the sort change, with the locale prefix and without the page, the loaded pages (`lm`) and the default sort, and from an SEO category to its original category; the new `useGetSortTarget()` in `utils/queryParams/useUpdateSortQuery.ts` gives that URL to both, and the new `getQueriesForVisibleUrl()` in `utils/queryParams/pushQueries.ts` cleans its queries
    - the mobile sort button shows "Sort:" with a short label from the new translation keys `Cheapest` and `Most expensive`, the list keeps the full labels; translate the keys for your other languages
    - below 1024px the product count is shown next to the page heading instead of the bar, so the sort button and the view toggle fit one row; `CollapsibleDescription` now requires `productsCount`; if you render `DeferredFilterAndSortingBar` on your own page, pass it to `CollapsibleDescription` or render `ProductsCount` (`components/Blocks/Product/ProductsList/ProductsCount.tsx`) next to the heading, otherwise the count is missing on mobile
    - `ProductListViewModeToggle` is a segmented control: its options are `size-10 vl:size-8` in a muted track with 2px padding, so the group is 44px high (36px from `vl`), and the pressed option is a raised white surface; review your overrides
- the `navigateWithArrows()`, `pressHomeKey()` and `pressEndKey()` helpers were removed from `vitest/utils/accessibility/keyboard-navigation.ts`; use `userEvent.keyboard()` in your tests instead
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
