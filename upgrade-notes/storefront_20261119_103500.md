#### storefront: form fields and the product list sorting now share one visual language ([#XXXX](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/XXXX))

- form fields and buttons are softer and fields are as high as buttons of the matching size
    - `--radius-button` is now `10px` and `--radius-input` follows it; `--radius-counter` and `--radius-tag` follow `--radius-input` and `--radius-checkbox` uses `--radius-md`; if you set your own radius tokens, check that buttons and fields still share one shape
    - `TextInput`, `Textarea`, `Select`, `MultiSelect` and `DatePicker` have a 1px border; hover darkens it, and a click, an error and a warning thicken it to 2px with an inset ring, so the content does not move; use `getFieldStateClassName()`, `getFieldHeightClassName()` and the `FieldSize` type from `components/Forms/Lib/fieldUtils.ts` for your own fields
    - fields take their height from the button scale: the default field is 44px below 1024px and 48px from 1024px (was 56px), the small field is 40px (was 48px), and the floating label is 12px; review your custom `h-*` and `pt-*` overrides on fields and labels, and the error icon offsets in your copy of `FormLineError`
    - a `large` button next to a default field needs no height override, so `ApplyCodeForm` and `NewsletterForm` use the default field size without `min-h-12`; this replaces the `min-h-12` advice from #5047, remove the override from your buttons placed next to a field
    - the options list of `Select` and `MultiSelect` is detached from the field (4px gap, own border and shadow); remove your `rounded-b-*` and `border-t-*` overrides passed in `listClassName` or `selectClassName` of `Select` and in `listClassName` or `inputClassName` of `MultiSelect`
    - `Spinbox` uses the field border (`outline-input-border-default`) and its decrease button no longer has the gray fill; check your own spinbox styling
    - `--color-input-border-warning` is now `orange-600` for a 3:1 contrast, and the new `--color-input-bg-disabled` gives disabled fields a gray background; set both if you override the input colors
    - the selected filter chip uses the `--color-tag-*` and `--radius-tag` tokens, whose hover and border values changed; check your overrides of these tokens
    - `twMergeCustom()` now knows the radius tokens of the theme, so a later `rounded-*` class overrides `rounded-input`, `rounded-button` and the other tokens; check your className overrides that combine them
    - run `pnpm compile-tailwind-for-admin` to regenerate `public/tailwind-for-admin/style.css`
- keyboard focus fills text fields like buttons and links, a click only thickens the border
    - the new `focus-keyboard` variant in `styles/base.css` matches a focused element only while the customer navigates with Tab; it reads `<html data-focus-source>`, which the new `useFocusSource()` from `utils/accessibility/useFocusSource.ts` keeps up to date
    - call `useFocusSource()` in your `pages/_app.tsx`, otherwise fields never get the keyboard focus fill
    - use `focus-keyboard:` instead of `focus:` for a focus fill on your own text fields
- the product list sorting bar is lighter and fits one row on mobile
    - from 1024px the options are tabs styled like the review sorting (`getSortingTabClassName()` in `components/Blocks/SortingBar/sortingBarUtils.ts` is shared by both); at most 4 options are visible, the rest is under "More" and the selected option always stays visible; in your tests, open "More" (`blocks_sortingbar_more_button`) before clicking an option after the fourth
    - the bar no longer has the full-width bottom border, and the sorting is not rendered when there is only one option; check your custom sort options
    - the desktop options are links in `<nav>` with `aria-current` instead of `role="listbox"` and `option` with `aria-selected`, and the mobile dropdown is a disclosure (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, no `aria-haspopup`) with links instead of `role="menu"` and `menuitem`; update your tests and selectors that rely on these roles
    - `SortingBarItem` and `SortingBarOptions` accept `variant: 'tab' | 'dropdown'` instead of `role` and `itemRole`, `SortingBarItem` requires `href` and `onClick`, and `MobileSortingActions` requires the new `sortOptionsShortLabels`; a click with a modifier key now keeps the browser behaviour (e.g. opens a new tab)
    - the mobile sort button shows "Sort:" with a short label from the new translation keys `Cheapest` and `Most expensive`, the list keeps the full labels; translate the keys for your other languages
    - below 1024px the product count is shown next to the page heading instead of the bar, so the sort button and the view toggle fit one row; if you render `DeferredFilterAndSortingBar` on your own page, pass `productsCount` to `CollapsibleDescription` or render `ProductsCount` (`components/Blocks/Product/ProductsList/ProductsCount.tsx`) next to the heading, otherwise the count is missing on mobile
    - `ProductListViewModeToggle` is a framed group and its buttons are `size-11 vl:size-9` with the pressed option marked by a background; review your overrides
- the `navigateWithArrows()`, `pressHomeKey()` and `pressEndKey()` helpers were removed from `vitest/utils/accessibility/keyboard-navigation.ts`; use `userEvent.keyboard()` in your tests instead
