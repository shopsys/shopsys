#### admin datagrid now narrows its records by a quick search, declarative filters and conditions ([#4857](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4857))

- `AdapterInterface::getDatasource()` now takes a single `DatasourceRequest` (the fields, the identification name and the condition of the listing) instead of `(string $identificationName, array $fields)`, and the interface gained `getExpressionCapabilities()` - update your own adapters, see `OrmAdapter` and `ArrayAdapter`
- `OrmAdapterFactory` and `ArrayAdapterFactory` have new constructor dependencies (`DqlExpressionBuilderFactoryInterface` / `PhpExpressionBuilder` and `ExpressionCompilerInterface`) - update the calls if you instantiate them yourself
- `ProxyQuery::addSelect()` is unchanged, but the class now also compiles conditions (`resolvePath()`, `addParameter()`, `applyExpression()`, `buildPredicate()`, `buildExistsPredicate()`, `describePath()`); `PartType` enum was removed in favour of `PathResolution`
- `Datagrid` constructor gained `ExpressionOperatorApplicability` and the options `domainControlScope`, `requestState` and `filterEnvironment`; `DatagridFactory` gained `DomainControlScopeFactory`, `DatagridRequestStateResolver`, `ExpressionOperatorApplicability`, `ExpressionOperatorEnum` and `DisplayTimeZoneProviderInterface` - create datagrids through the factory
- `Datagrid` has new methods `addCondition()`, `filters()`, `getDomainControlScope()`, `getRequestState()`, `getQuickSearch()`, `getFilterForm()` and `hasFilterRules()`; a field may now be marked `'searchable' => true` to join the quick search
- the domain control of a CRUD list moved from the controller to the datagrid:
    - `CrudListDomainControl` was replaced by `DomainControlType` (`FILTER`, `SWITCHER`, `NONE`) and `CrudConfig::setListDomainControl()` now takes `(DomainControlType $type, ?array $allowedDomainIds = null, ?string $filterNamespace = null)`; `CrudConfigData::getListDomainControl()` and `getListAllowedDomainIds()` were replaced by `getDomainControlConfig()`
    - `AbstractCrudController` lost the public properties `$domain`, `$adminDomainTabsFacade` and `$adminDomainFilterTabsFacade` and the methods `getSelectedListDomainId()`, `getListDomainFilterNamespace()`, `getListDomainIds()`, `getEffectiveListDomainIds()`, `addListDomainIdsCondition()` and `applyListDomainFilter()` - the domain condition is applied by the datagrid for entities implementing `DomainSeparatedEntityInterface`, other entities get no domain control; replace your own domain conditions in `configureQuery()` by `$datagrid->addCondition()` and use `$datagrid->getDomainControlScope()->isDomainWorthDisplaying()` instead of `$this->domain->isMultidomain()` to decide about the domain column
    - `templates/crud/list.html.twig` renders the domain control, the quick search and the filters in the new `datagrid_controls` block through the `Admin:Grid:DomainControl`, `Admin:Grid:QuickSearch` and `Admin:Grid:Filters` components; the variables `listDomainControl`, `listDomainFilterNamespace` and `listDomainIds` were replaced by `domainControlScope`, `quickSearch` and `filterForm` - update your overridden list templates
- `configureQuery()` is meant for the static shape of the query only (computed columns, joins); a fixed condition belongs to `$datagrid->addCondition(Condition::equals('deleted', false))` and a condition driven by the administrator to a filter (`$datagrid->filters()->add(TextFilter::new('author.fullName', t('Author')))`), see the [Narrowing the records](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/administration/datagrid/narrowing/) and [Filters](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/administration/datagrid/filters/) documentation
- the string comparisons of the datagrid (quick search, `TextFilter`) are diacritics-insensitive thanks to the `NORMALIZED()` DQL function
- the quick search, the filter and the domain control of a CRUD list share one toolbar above the grid - update your overridden templates:
    - `templates/crud/list.html.twig` wraps the controls and the grid in `.datagrid-shell`; the quick search and a button opening the filter share one row of `.datagrid-toolbar`, and the `Admin:Grid:ActiveFilters` component spells out every applied rule on a chip below them, with the words combining them between the chips; a chip is a link taking its own rule back - the tabs switching between the quick search and the filter are gone
    - the filter form moved into a panel sliding in from the side of the page (a Bootstrap offcanvas), rendered by the new `datagrid_filter_panel` block of the list template
    - `Admin:Grid:DomainControl` renders the domain filter as `nav nav-tabs` (`components/grid/domain_tabs.html.twig`) and picks the dropdown (`components/grid/domain_dropdown.html.twig`) itself above `shopsys_administration.datagrid.max_domains_rendered_as_tabs` domains (six by default) or in a narrow window; the `select` property of `DomainControlComponent` was removed, drop it from your `component('Admin:Grid:DomainControl', ...)` calls. The pages of the administration keep rendering `partial/quick_domain_filter.html.twig` and `partial/quick_domain_filter_select.html.twig` unchanged
    - `Admin:Grid:QuickSearch` renders its own input group with a cross clearing the searched text instead of including `partial/_quick_search_form.html.twig`, which stays as it was for the pages of the administration
    - `templates/components/grid/filters.html.twig` renders the logical operators as button groups instead of selects and shows an empty state with a single "Add condition" action; the `datagrid-filter` Stimulus controller gained the `emptyState` and `addGroup` targets
- taking back a single composed rule is new: `DatagridNarrowingUrlFactory::createUrlWithoutFilterRule()` cuts one rule out of the submitted filter, drops the group that held it when it was its last rule and the whole filter when that was its last group
- the "Reset filter" links of a datagrid work again: the quick search and the filter travel in top level query parameters, which `GridView::getUrl()` cannot drop (everything it is given lands inside the `g[<gridId>]` namespace), so they are built by the new `DatagridNarrowingUrlFactory`
- `shopsys_administration.datagrid.max_domains_rendered_as_tabs` is a new configuration option (six by default)
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
