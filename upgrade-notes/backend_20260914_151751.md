#### admin datagrid now narrows its records by a quick search, declarative filters and conditions ([#4857](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4857))

- `AdapterInterface::getDatasource()` now takes a single `DatasourceRequest` (the fields, the identification name and the condition of the listing) instead of `(string $identificationName, array $fields)` - update your own adapters, see `OrmAdapter` and `ArrayAdapter`; the builder of a medium extends the new `AbstractExpressionBuilder`, whose `compile()` turns the condition of the request into the expression of the medium
- `OrmAdapterFactory` and `OrmAdapter` have new constructor dependencies `DqlExpressionBuilderFactoryInterface` and `PathWalkerInterface`, `ArrayAdapterFactory` and `ArrayAdapter` a new constructor dependency `ExpressionBuilderInterface` (aliased to `PhpExpressionBuilder`), and `ProxyQuery` takes `PathWalkerInterface` after the entity manager - update the calls if you instantiate them yourself; alias `PathWalkerInterface` to your own walker to read the paths differently (implemented by `PathWalker`)
- `ProxyQuery::addSelect()` is unchanged, but the class now also compiles conditions (`resolvePath()`, `addParameter()`, `exists()`, `applyExpression()`); `PartType` enum was removed in favour of `PathResolution`
- `Datagrid` constructor gained `DatagridNarrowingResolver`, `DatagridRequestState` and `?DomainControlScope` before the options; `DatagridFactory` gained `DomainControlScopeFactory`, `DatagridNarrowingResolver` and `RequestStack` - create datagrids through the factory; `DatagridFactory::create()` takes the optional `?DatagridRequestState $requestState` (see `DatagridRequestState::fromRequest()` and `outsideRequest()`) and `?DomainControlScope $domainControlScope` after the options
- `Datagrid` has new methods `filters()`, `getDomainControlScope()` and `getNarrowing()`; the latter returns the domain control, the quick search and the filter panel resolved for the request together with the condition they combine into (`DatagridNarrowing`, resolved by the `DatagridNarrowingResolver` service from `SearchablePathResolver`, `FilterSetResolver` and `DatagridFormFactory` - replace them to narrow differently)
- a field may now be marked `'searchable' => true` to join the quick search and `'filterable' => true` to get a filter picked by the value of its path (`FilterGuesser`, replace the service to pick differently), a filter declared by `filters()->add()` under the name of the field takes precedence; a field restricted by `role` or `permission` is neither searched nor filtered by an administrator without the permission, and neither is a filter declared under its name or over one of its properties
- `ProductReviewController` has a new constructor dependency `ProductReviewStatusEnum` and declares its filters in the new protected method `configureFilters()` - update the constructor call if you extend the controller
- the domain control of a CRUD list moved from the controller to the datagrid:
    - `CrudListDomainControl` was replaced by `DomainControlType` (`FILTER`, `SWITCHER`, `NONE`) and `CrudConfig::setListDomainControl()` now takes `(DomainControlType $domainControlType, ?array $allowedDomainIds = null, ?string $filterNamespace = null, ?string $domainIdPath = null)`; `CrudConfigData::getListDomainControl()` and `getListAllowedDomainIds()` were replaced by `getDomainControlConfig()`
    - `AbstractCrudController` lost the public properties `$domain`, `$adminDomainTabsFacade` and `$adminDomainFilterTabsFacade` and the methods `getSelectedListDomainId()`, `getListDomainFilterNamespace()`, `getListDomainIds()`, `getEffectiveListDomainIds()`, `addListDomainIdsCondition()` and `applyListDomainFilter()` - the datagrid limits a list of an entity implementing `DomainSeparatedEntityInterface` to the domains of its domain control and adds a `domainId` column when the list works with several domains; remove your own domain conditions and domain columns from such lists; limit a list of an entity related to a domain in another way by the path to its domain instead of your own condition - `setListDomainControl(DomainControlType::FILTER, domainIdPath: 'domains.domainId')`
    - a list of an entity implementing `DomainSeparatedEntityInterface` now displays the domain filter even without `setListDomainControl()` - call `setListDomainControl(DomainControlType::NONE)` in `configure()` to keep such a list without a domain control
    - `templates/crud/list.html.twig` renders the domain control, the quick search and the filters in the new `datagrid_controls` block (replacing the `domain_control` block) through the `Admin:Grid:DomainControl`, `Admin:Grid:QuickSearch` and `Admin:Grid:Filters` components; the variables `listDomainControl`, `listDomainFilterNamespace` and `listDomainIds` were replaced by `domainControlScope`, `quickSearch`, `filterForm`, `filterPrototypesForm` (passed to the `prototypesForm` prop of `Admin:Grid:Filters`) and `filterActive` (whether a composed filter outranks the quick search, passed to its `active` prop) - update your overridden list templates
- `configureQuery()` keeps the static shape of the query (fixed conditions, computed columns, joins); a condition driven by the administrator belongs to a filter (`$datagrid->filters()->add(TextFilter::new('author.fullName', t('Author')))`), see the [Narrowing the records](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/administration/datagrid/narrowing/) and [Filters](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/administration/datagrid/filters/) documentation
- the string comparisons of the datagrid (quick search, `TextFilter`) are diacritics-insensitive thanks to the `NORMALIZED()` DQL function
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
