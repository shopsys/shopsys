#### admin datagrid now narrows its records by a quick search, declarative filters and conditions ([#4857](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4857))

- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Adapter\AdapterInterface::getDatasource()` now takes a single `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Adapter\DatasourceRequest` (the identification name, the fields and the condition of the listing) instead of `(string $identificationName, array $fields)` - update your own adapters
    - apply `$request->condition` to the records — it carries the domain limit of the list as well, so an adapter ignoring it lists the records of every domain; compile it by a builder of your medium extending `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Expression\AbstractExpressionBuilder`, see `OrmAdapter` and `ArrayAdapter`
- a join of a nested association of the datagrid query is aliased by the whole path to it (`currency__format_join` instead of `format_join`) - update your own DQL referring to such an alias
- create datagrids by `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\DatagridFactory::create()`, the `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Datagrid` itself needs the state of the request and the domain control of the list
- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Datagrid::add()`, `update()` and `remove()` throw once the narrowing of the datagrid is resolved by `getNarrowing()` or `createView()` - configure the datagrid before you ask for its view
- `@ShopsysAdministration/crud/list.html.twig` renders the domain control, the quick search and the filters in the new `datagrid_controls` block, fed by the new template variables `domainControlScope`, `quickSearch`, `filterForm`, `filterPrototypesForm` and `filterActive` - render the block in your overridden list templates
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Component\Paginator\QueryPaginator::getTotalNativeQuery()` passes the `Doctrine\ORM\Query\Parameter` objects themselves to `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Component\Doctrine\SqlParametersFlattener::flattenArrayParameters()`, so that the count query binds every value by its type - if you have extended the flattener, keep the values as they are
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Form\Transformers\AbstractEntityIdToEntityTransformer` refuses an identifier greater than its `MAXIMUM_ENTITY_ID` (the greatest integer of the database) before looking the entity up - override the constant in your transformer of an entity identified by a big integer
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
