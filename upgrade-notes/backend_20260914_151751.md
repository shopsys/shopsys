#### admin datagrid now narrows its records by a quick search, declarative filters and conditions ([#4857](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4857))

- a CRUD list of an entity implementing `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Component\Domain\Entity\DomainSeparatedEntityInterface` now displays a domain filter and lists only the records of the selected domain, or of the domains the administrator works with, even without calling `setListDomainControl()`, see [CRUD controller reference](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/administration/crud-controller/reference/crud-controller/#list-domain-control) for details
    - remove the manual domain conditions from `configureQuery()` of such CRUD controllers
    - call `$config->setListDomainControl(DomainControlType::NONE)` (`Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\DomainControl\DomainControlType`) in `configure()` to keep such a list without a domain control
- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Config\CrudConfig::setListDomainControl()` now takes `DomainControlType` instead of the removed `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Config\CrudListDomainControl` - replace `CrudListDomainControl::QUICK_FILTER` by `DomainControlType::FILTER` and `CrudListDomainControl::SWITCHER` by `DomainControlType::SWITCHER`
    - its new `$filterNamespace` parameter shares the selection of the filter with another list - pass it instead of overriding `AbstractCrudController::getListDomainFilterNamespace()`
    - its new `$domainIdPath` parameter limits a list of an entity related to a domain in another way by the path to its domain (`setListDomainControl(DomainControlType::FILTER, domainIdPath: 'domains.domainId')`) - pass it instead of calling `addListDomainIdsCondition()` in `configureQuery()`
- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Config\CrudConfigData::__construct()` takes `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\DomainControl\DomainControlConfig $domainControlConfig` instead of `$listDomainControl` and `$listAllowedDomainIds`, and its methods `getListDomainControl()` and `getListAllowedDomainIds()` were replaced by `getDomainControlConfig()`
- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Controller\AbstractCrudController` no longer knows the domain - its public properties `$domain`, `$adminDomainTabsFacade` and `$adminDomainFilterTabsFacade` and its methods `getSelectedListDomainId()`, `getListDomainFilterNamespace()`, `getListDomainIds()`, `getEffectiveListDomainIds()`, `addListDomainIdsCondition()` and `applyListDomainFilter()` were removed
    - to read the domain control in `configureQuery()`, inject `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\DomainControl\DomainControlScopeFactory` and call `create($this->definition->getConfig()->getDomainControlConfig(), $this->definition->entityClass)` - the returned scope holds `$selectedDomainId`, `$filterNamespace`, `$domainIds` and `getEffectiveDomainIds()`, and it is `null` for a list without a domain control; the domain condition itself is applied by the datagrid
    - instead of overriding `applyListDomainFilter()` with an empty body, call `$config->setListDomainControl(DomainControlType::NONE)` in `configure()` - it hides the domain control as well
    - instead of overriding `applyListDomainFilter()` with a condition of your own, pass the path to the domain by the `$domainIdPath` parameter of `setListDomainControl()`
- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Controller\AbstractCrudController` has the new public `#[Required]` property `$datagridStateFactory` (`Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\State\DatagridStateFactory`) - remove a property of the same name from your CRUD controllers and use the inherited one
- the `domain_control` block of `@ShopsysAdministration/crud/list.html.twig` and its template variables `listDomainControl`, `listDomainFilterNamespace` and `listDomainIds` were removed
    - Twig silently skips a block the parent template no longer has - move a `domain_control` block that added content around the domain control into the new `datagrid_controls` block (keep `{{ parent() }}`), and override `@ShopsysAdministration/components/grid/domain_control.html.twig` to replace the domain control itself
- every list of an entity implementing `DomainSeparatedEntityInterface` now gets the `domainId` field automatically - the datagrid adds it after `configureDatagrid()` when the domain filter offers more than one domain, see [Automatic domain field](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/administration/crud-controller/reference/crud-controller/#automatic-domain-field)
    - a field you add under the name `domainId` takes precedence over the automatic one
    - a domain column of your own under another name (`->add('domain', ['property' => 'domainId'])`) is displayed next to the automatic one - rename your field to `domainId`, or remove it
    - `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Controller\PriceListController` and `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Controller\ProductReviewController` no longer add the `domainId` field themselves, so `$datagrid->update('domainId', ...)` in your extension of these controllers fails - call `$datagrid->add('domainId', ...)` instead
- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Controller\ProductReviewController::__construct()` takes the new `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\ProductReview\ProductReviewStatusEnum $productReviewStatusEnum` and `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Controller\StoreController::__construct()` the new `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Component\Domain\AdminDomainTabsFacade $adminDomainTabsFacade` - update your classes extending them
- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Adapter\AdapterInterface::getDatasource()` now takes a single `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Adapter\DatasourceRequest` (the identification name, the fields and the condition of the listing) instead of `(string $identificationName, array $fields)` - update your own adapters
    - apply `$request->condition` to the records — it carries the domain limit of the list as well, so an adapter ignoring it lists the records of every domain; compile it by a builder of your adapter extending `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Expression\AbstractExpressionBuilder`, see `OrmAdapter` and `ArrayAdapter`
- the constructors of the datagrid adapters take new dependencies - pass them if you create the classes yourself instead of using their factories:
    - `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Adapter\Array\ArrayAdapter` and `ArrayAdapterFactory` take `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Expression\ExpressionBuilderInterface $expressionBuilder`
    - `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Adapter\Orm\OrmAdapter` and `OrmAdapterFactory` take `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Adapter\Orm\Expression\DqlExpressionBuilderFactoryInterface $dqlExpressionBuilderFactory` and `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Adapter\Orm\PathWalkerInterface $pathWalker` (in `OrmAdapter` before `$configureQuery`)
    - `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Adapter\Orm\ProxyQuery` takes `PathWalkerInterface $pathWalker` before `$locale`
- a join of a nested association of the datagrid query is aliased by the whole path to it (`currency__format_join` instead of `format_join`) - update your own DQL referring to such an alias
- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Datagrid::createView()` now requires the state of the datagrid - once the datagrid is configured, create it by `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\State\DatagridStateFactory::create($datagrid->getConfig())` and pass it, see [Narrowing the records](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/administration/datagrid/narrowing/#a-datagrid-outside-the-crud-controller)
    - the state carries the quick search, the filter and the domain control of the datagrid; answer 422 when `$datagridState->isInvalid()` and render the controls by `component('Admin:Grid:Controls', { state: datagridState, gridView: grid })`
    - a datagrid outside CRUD gets a domain control by the new `domainControl` option of `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\DatagridFactory::create()`
- `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Datagrid::add()`, `update()`, `remove()` and `filters()` throw once the configuration of the datagrid is frozen by `getConfig()` - configure the datagrid before you create its state
- `@ShopsysAdministration/crud/list.html.twig` renders the domain control, the quick search and the filters in the new `datagrid_controls` block by the `Admin:Grid:Controls` component, fed by the new template variable `datagridState` - render the block in your overridden list templates, and rename a template parameter named `datagridState` you set in `configureTemplateParameters()` of a CRUD controller or its extension
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Component\Paginator\QueryPaginator::getTotalNativeQuery()` passes the `Doctrine\ORM\Query\Parameter` objects themselves to `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Component\Doctrine\SqlParametersFlattener::flattenArrayParameters()`, so that the count query binds every value by its type - if you have extended the flattener, keep the values as they are
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Form\Transformers\AbstractEntityIdToEntityTransformer` refuses an identifier greater than its `MAXIMUM_ENTITY_ID` (2147483647, the maximum of an `integer` column), or a negative one, before looking the entity up - override the constant in your transformer of an entity identified by a big integer
- the `excluded_class_names` option of `Shopsys\CodingStandards\CsFixer\ExtendedClassNameResolverFixer` now excludes the descendants and the implementations of the listed classes as well, and excludes `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Filter\FilterInterface` and `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Component\Datagrid\Filter\Form\Data\FilterFormData` by default - if you configure your own `excluded_class_names`, add these two to them
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
