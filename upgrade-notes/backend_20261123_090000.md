#### Fix compilation of the container with scheb/2fa-bundle 7.15.0 and newer ([#5061](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5061))

- class `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\DependencyInjection\ServicesResetter` was removed and the feed generation no longer resets services after each batch, the memory is freed by clearing the entity manager, which also clears `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Component\Cache\InMemoryCache`
    - if your service implementing `Symfony\Contracts\Service\ResetInterface` relied on being reset during the feed generation, store its cached data in `InMemoryCache` instead
