#### Advanced search moved

In an effort to make the structure of the framework better, the namespace `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\AdvancedSearch` now houses only shared classes used throughout the framework and your project. This meant moving product, order and complaint advanced search related classes to their respective namespaces in keeping with the established domain-driven design. Some classes were also renamed to keep a consistent pattern.

For example, class `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\AdvancedSearchOrder\AdvancedSearchOrderFacade` was moved and renamed as `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Order\AdvancedSearch\OrderAdvancedSearchFacade`.

#### New abstract facade

Abstract class `AbstractAdvancedSearchFacade` was introduced to make creating new advanced search easier. Make your custom facades extend it and compare their methods - most likely you won't need your implementation, as they share common logic. The absolute minimal effort required is implementing the new `getDefaultFilterName` method.
