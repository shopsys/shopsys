#### storefront: text font roles, round status icons, a single label for additional services and product variants on the new scales ([#5047](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5047))

- storefront text uses Inter, Raleway 600 stays for headings from 18px, buttons and the CMS button
    - `--font-weight-*: initial` in `styles/theme.css` keeps only `font-normal` (400), `font-medium` (500), `font-semibold` (600) and `font-bold` (700); `font-light`, `font-thin`, `font-extralight`, `font-extrabold` and `font-black` no longer apply, so replace them in your components
    - 400 carries text, 500 the interface and text actions, 600 headings, buttons and badges and 700 prices only; Inter loads all four from one variable file
    - the new role tokens `font-heading`, `font-action`, `font-ui`, `font-product-name` and `font-price` set the weight of headings, buttons (`font-action`), interface labels and text actions (`font-ui`: menu, tabs, tags, availability, USP and category names, `Link` and `TextButton` actions), product names (500) and prices in one place; `twMergeCustom()` treats them as font weights
    - Raleway loads only 600, and bold text in content and the `h5` and `h6` headings use Inter 600
    - `Fonts` defines `--font-inter` and `--font-raleway` on `:root`, so `font-default` and `font-secondary` also work in portals and inside the other font; the `ralewayFont` export of `components/Pages/App/Fonts.tsx` and its class on the `AppPageContent` wrapper were removed
- success and error states use round icons from one set
    - `CheckmarkDoneIcon` was renamed to `CheckmarkRoundIcon`, `RejectedIcon` was removed and the new `CloseRoundIcon` shows errors; the plain `CheckmarkIcon` and `CloseIcon` stay for selection and closing
    - the `StatusBadge` icon is now 16px
- an additional service checkbox now has a single label, the service name, stretched over the whole item, so the checkbox and the image still toggle it
    - the image and the delivery extension are no longer labels (`AdditionalServiceImageLabel` is now `AdditionalServiceImage`), and the delivery extension describes the checkbox together with the price; `AdditionalServiceLabel` requires the new `deliveryExtensionId`
    - the checkbox and radio indicator is the new `ChoiceIndicator` (`components/Forms/Lib/ChoiceIndicator.tsx`), which `LabelWrapper` renders too
- semantic tokens that were never defined are replaced, so these styles apply again: the selected gallery thumbnail uses `border-border-active`, the active blog category `text-text-accent`, the photo count overlay `bg-white-alpha-100`, and the review stars `--color-rating-empty`; the banner title keeps dark text on the orange keyboard focus
- `vitest/conventions/linkClassNames.test.ts` reports hand-written link styles behind any variant chain (e.g. `lg:hover:underline`, `group-hover/title:underline`) and with the `!` modifier, and fails for a file in `OWN_LINK_STYLE_PATHS` that no longer styles its links by hand; check your exceptions
- the header phone changes only its color on hover and its `simple_header_contact` TID now reaches the anchor, a cart dropdown item underlines its product name on hover and a footer contact card darkens its surface
- the product variants from #4978 follow the new scales: the "Choose a variant" button of a main variant is `xlarge` like the add to cart button, the variants popup rows, the comparison variant group and the main variant purchase box use the role radii, and variant names use `font-product-name`
- the add to cart button in the sticky product detail bar is `medium` instead of `large`
- the product detail section navigation uses the tabs of the sorting (`getSortingTabClassName()`) instead of `Tag`, with `aria-current="location"` on the active section, and its skeleton shows the text tabs; the sorting tabs keep one weight (`font-ui`) in both states, so update your tests that relied on the active tab being semibold
- forms in popups no longer sit on the gray surface: `FormBlockWrapper` has the new `variant="plain"`, used by the delivery address, complaint, customer user, watchdog, product question, product review and inquiry popups, which are now `size="small"`; a small popup has the smaller `h4` title
    - popup actions use the new `PopupActions` (`components/Layout/Popup/PopupActions.tsx`): aligned right with the secondary action first and stacked on mobile; the form popups have a Cancel button next to the submit button, which is no longer `large`, and the merge carts, delete delivery address and pickup place popups no longer spread their actions to the edges
    - a scrolling popup content with a plain form uses `-mx-1 px-1`, so it does not clip the hover rings and focus outlines
- selected filters only fade in and out, a removed group no longer slides over the filter panel; `AnimateSlideDiv` and `slideAnimation` were removed, use `fadeAnimation` or your own variants if you used them
- user text is sized for descriptions: `.user-text` (category and product descriptions, popups) is 16px with a smaller heading scale and spacing, articles and blog posts rendered by `GrapesJs` add the new `.user-text-large` with the previous 18px from `md`; paragraphs inside user text follow its size and line height instead of the `p` ones from `styles/base.css`; add `.user-text-large` where your own long reads should stay larger
- the product name in the cart, the add to cart popup and the customer's reviews is underlined on hover like in the cart dropdown, instead of changing color or not reacting
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
