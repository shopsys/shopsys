#### rewrite easy controllers to crud controller ([#4848](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4848))

- the legacy admin controllers were replaced by CRUD controllers in `Shopsys\AdministrationBundle\Controller` and their route names changed, update your links and redirects (the URL leaf segments changed too, e.g. `/list/` → `/`, `/new/` → `/create`)
    - `admin_parametergroup_*` → `admin_crud_parameter_group_*` (`ParameterGroupController`)
    - `admin_navigation_*` → `admin_crud_navigation_*` (`NavigationController`)
    - `admin_notificationbar_*` → `admin_crud_notification_bar_*` (`NotificationBarController`)
    - `admin_slider_*` → `admin_crud_slider_*` (`SliderController`), the `/item/` URL segment was dropped
    - `admin_advert_*` → `admin_crud_advert_*` (`AdvertController`)
    - `admin_salesrepresentative_*` → `admin_crud_sales_representative_*` (`SalesRepresentativeController`), the `deleteconfirm` route was removed, the confirm message is rendered by the datagrid
    - `admin_closedday_*` → `admin_crud_closed_day_*` (`ClosedDayController`), `admin_closedday_holidaysimport` → `admin_crud_closed_day_holidays_import`
    - `admin_store_*` → `admin_crud_store_*` (`StoreController`), `admin_store_setdefault` → `admin_crud_store_set_default`, `admin_store_loadcoordinates` → `admin_crud_store_load_coordinates`
    - `admin_pricelist_*` → `admin_crud_price_list_*` (`PriceListController`), `admin_pricelist_export|import|loadmetadata` → `admin_crud_price_list_export|import|load_metadata`
- the following classes were removed, if you extended them, move your changes to a `#[CrudControllerExtension]` class (see [Extending existing CRUD Controller](https://docs.shopsys.com/en/latest/administration/crud-controller/getting-started/extending-existing-crud-controller/))
    - `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Controller\Admin\{ParameterGroup,Navigation,NotificationBar,Slider,Advert,SalesRepresentative,ClosedDay,Store,PriceList}Controller`
    - `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\SalesRepresentative\SalesRepresentativeGridFactory`
    - `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Store\ClosedDay\Grid\ClosedDayGridFactory`
    - `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\PriceList\PriceListGridFactory`
- the grid query helpers of the converted sections were removed as they are replaced by the CRUD list query: `ParameterGroupFacade::getOrderedParameterGroupsQueryBuilder()`, `ParameterRepository::getOrderedParameterGroupsQueryBuilder()`, `NotificationBarFacade::getAllByDomainIdQueryBuilderForGrid()`, `NotificationBarRepository::getAllByDomainIdQueryBuilderForGrid()`, `SalesRepresentativeFacade::getAllQueryBuilder()`, `StoreFacade::getStoresByDomainIdQueryBuilder()`, `PriceListFacade::getPriceListGridQueryBuilder()`, `PriceListRepository::getPriceListGridQueryBuilder()`
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Customer\User\CustomerUserFacade::findEmailsOfCustomerUsersUsingSalesRepresentative()` and `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Customer\User\CustomerUserRepository::findEmailsOfCustomerUsersUsingSalesRepresentative()` were replaced by `getEmailsOfCustomerUsersIndexedBySalesRepresentativeId(array $salesRepresentativeIds)`
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Store\StoreFacade::delete()` now throws `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Store\Exception\DefaultStoreCannotBeDeletedException` for the default store, handle it where your project calls the method directly
- the `list`, `listGrid`, `new`, `edit` and `detail` templates under `@ShopsysAdministration/content/{parameterGroup,navigation,notificationBar,slider,advert,salesRepresentative,closedDay,store,priceList}/` were removed, the pages are rendered by the CRUD templates
- the `SideMenuBuilder` constants of the converted sections were removed (`LIST_/NEW_/EDIT_PARAMETER_GROUP`, `LIST_/NEW_/EDIT_NAVIGATION`, `LIST_/NEW_/EDIT_NOTIFICATION_BAR`, `LIST_/NEW_/EDIT_BANNER`, `LIST_/NEW_/EDIT_ADVERT`, `LIST_/NEW_/EDIT_SALES_REPRESENTATIVE`, `LIST_/NEW_/EDIT_CLOSED_DAY`, `LIST_/NEW_/EDIT_STORE`, `LIST_/NEW_/EDIT_PRICE_LIST`), the menu items are now registered by the CRUD controllers under their list route name (e.g. `admin_crud_store_list`)
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
