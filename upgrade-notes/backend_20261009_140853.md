#### FE API: product filter options are batch loaded ([#4938](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4938))

- `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Model\Product\Connection\ProductConnection::getProductFilterOptions()` now returns `ProductFilterOptions|Promise`, resolve the promise if you call the method directly
- `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Model\Product\Connection\ProductConnectionFactory::getProductFilterOptionsClosure()` was replaced by the protected `createProductFilterOptionsClosure(ProductFilterOptionsBatchLoadData $batchLoadData)`
- `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Model\Product\Filter\ProductFilterOptionsFactory`: `createProductFilterOptionsForAll()`, `createProductFilterOptionsForCategory()`, `createProductFilterOptionsForBrand()`, and `createProductFilterOptionsForFlag()` were replaced by `createProductFilterOptionsByBatchLoadData()`, `fillParametersForCategory()` accepts the collapsed parameters (`array $collapsedParameters`) instead of `Category $category`
- `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Model\Product\Filter\ProductFilterFacade::getProductFilterConfigForSearch()` was removed, use `getProductFilterConfigsByBatchLoadData()`
- check the flag and brand choices of the brand, search, and all-products listings if your project relied on the former database queries, their `ProductFilterConfig` is now built from Elasticsearch aggregations the same way as the category and flag ones
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Filter\ProductFilterConfigFactory`: `createForCategory()`, `createForSearch()`, `createForBrand()`, `createForAll()`, and `createForFlag()` were replaced by `createByBatchLoadData(array $batchLoadDataIndexedByKey, string $locale)`
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Filter\FlagFilterChoiceRepository`, `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Filter\BrandFilterChoiceRepository`, and `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Filter\PriceRangeRepository` were removed
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\ProductRepository`: `getListableInCategoryQueryBuilder()`, `getListableForBrandQueryBuilder()`, `getSellableInCategoryQueryBuilder()`, `getListableBySearchTextQueryBuilder()`, and `filterByBrand()` were removed
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Search\ProductElasticsearchRepository`: `filterBySearchText()`, `getFoundProductIds()`, and `getProductIdsBySearchText()` were removed
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Filter\ProductFilterElasticFacade`: `getProductFilterDataInCategory()`, `getProductFilterDataForSearch()`, `getProductFilterDataInBrand()`, `getProductFilterDataInFlag()`, and `getProductFilterDataForAll()` were replaced by `getProductFilterConfigIdsDataByBatchLoadData()`
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\ProductOnCurrentDomainElasticFacade`: `getProductFilterCountDataInCategory()`, `getProductFilterCountDataForBrand()`, `getProductFilterCountDataForSearch()`, `getProductFilterCountDataForAll()`, and `getProductFilterCountDataForFlag()` were replaced by `getProductFilterCountDataByBatchLoadData()`
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Search\ProductFilterCountDataElasticsearchRepository`: `getProductFilterCountDataInSearch()` and `getProductFilterCountDataInCategory()` were replaced by `getProductFilterCountDataByRequests()`, the protected `calculateFlagsPlusNumbers()`, `calculateBrandsPlusNumbers()`, `replaceParametersPlusNumbers()`, and `calculateParameterPlusNumbers()` were replaced by `createSearchQueriesIndexedByQueryName()` and `createCountData()`
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Parameter\ParameterFacade`: `getParameterFilterChoicesByIds()` and `getParametersIdsSortedByPositionFilteredByCategory()` were replaced by `getParameterFilterChoicesByIdsIndexedByKey()` and `getParameterIdsSortedByPositionIndexedByCategoryId()`
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Category\CategoryParameterFacade::getParametersCollapsedByCategory()` was replaced by `getParametersCollapsedIndexedByCategoryId()`
- `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Component\Elasticsearch\MultipleSearchQuery` and `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Component\Elasticsearch\MultipleSearchQueryFactory` were removed, use `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Component\Elasticsearch\MultipleSearchFacade`
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
