#### Complete structured data for organizations, products and articles ([#4826](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4826))

- add the `OrganizationSetting` GraphQL type inheriting from `OrganizationSettingDecorator` to your project configuration, as `SeoSettingDecorator` now references it
- if you instantiate `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Article\ArticleData` directly and pass it to `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Article\ArticleFacade::create()` or `edit()`, initialize its `$image` using `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Component\FileUpload\ImageUploadDataFactory::create()` or create the article data through `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Article\ArticleDataFactory::create()`
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
