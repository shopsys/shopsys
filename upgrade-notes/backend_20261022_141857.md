#### Prevent customer managers from granting stronger role groups ([#4975](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/4975))

- review the customer user role groups of your project in the administration, a customer user can now assign a role group (`customerUserRoleGroups` query, `roleGroupUuid` of `AddNewCustomerUser` and `EditCustomerUserPersonalData` mutations) and edit or remove a customer user of that role group only when the role group's roles, expanded through the role hierarchy, are a subset of their own roles (see `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Customer\User\Role\CustomerUserRoleResolver::canCustomerUserAssignRoleGroup()`)
    - add the missing roles to the role groups of customer users who should be able to assign the other role groups
    - add `ROLE_API_ALL` to the role groups of customer users who should be able to assign a role group with `ROLE_API_ALL`, having all the individual roles is not enough
- if you redefine the `roleGroupUuid` field of `AddNewCustomerUserDataInput` or `EditCustomerUserPersonalDataInput` in your project, add the `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Component\Constraints\CustomerUserRoleGroupAssignable` constraint to its validation
- `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Voter\CustomerUserVoter::isEditedCustomerUserFromSameCompany()` was replaced by `canLoggedCustomerUserManageEditedCustomerUser()`, which grants access by `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Customer\User\Role\CustomerUserRoleResolver::canCustomerUserManageCustomerUser()`, update your overridden voter accordingly
- if you override `Shopsys\FrontendApiBundle\Model\Resolver\Customer\CustomerUserRoleGroup\CustomerUserRoleGroupQuery::customerUserRoleGroupsQuery()`, use `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Customer\User\Role\CustomerUserRoleGroupFacade::getAllAssignableByCustomerUser()` instead of `getAll()`
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
