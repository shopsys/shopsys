#### Stop resending orders rejected by Packetery ([#5070](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5070))

- `createPacket` now always sends `currency` (the order currency code) along with `value`; previously Packeta assumed the destination country's currency
    - if you send Packetery orders in a currency other than the destination country's local currency (e.g. EUR orders delivered to CZ), enable that currency in "Currency conversions" in the Packeta client section, otherwise Packeta rejects the packet with "You entered an unauthorized currency"
- if you override `@ShopsysFramework/Packetery/createPacket.xml.twig`, add `<currency>{{ packet.currency }}</currency>` to it
