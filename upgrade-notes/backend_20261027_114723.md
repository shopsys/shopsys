#### Recalculation SQL queries rewrite only changed rows ([#5003](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5003))

- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\ProductVisibilityRepository`: the protected methods `calculateIndependentVisibility()`, `hideVariantsWithInvisibleMainVariant()` and `hideMainVariantsWithoutVisibleVariants()` were replaced by a single statement built in `getVisibilityRecalculationSql()` and executed in `recalculateVisibility()`
    - move your own independent visibility conditions into an override of `getIndependentVisibilityConditionSql()` appended to `parent::getIndependentVisibilityConditionSql()`, and add your parameters to `getIndependentVisibilityParameters()` and `getIndependentVisibilityParameterTypes()` while keeping the parent ones
    - the condition is evaluated for the `product_visibilities` rows of all domains and pricing groups at once with the aliases `pv` (`product_visibilities`), `p` (`products`), `pd` (`product_domains`), `pg` (`pricing_groups`) and `dl` (domain locales); replace `:locale` with `dl.locale`, `:domainId` with `pv.domain_id` and `:pricingGroupId` with `pv.pricing_group_id`
    - move your changes of the variant rules into an override of `getVisibilityRecalculationSql()`
    - `refreshProductsVisibility()` and the protected method `recalculateVisibility()` now return the IDs of the products whose visibility changed when product IDs are given, and the statement from `getVisibilityRecalculationSql()` has to select them; update your overrides accordingly
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\ProductVisibilityFacade::calculateProductVisibilityForIds()` now returns the IDs of the products whose visibility changed, return them from your override as well
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\ProductSellingDeniedRecalculator`: the protected methods `calculate()`, `calculatePerDomain()`, `propagateMainVariantSellingDeniedToVariants()` and `propagateVariantsSellingDeniedToMainVariant()` were replaced by a single statement built in `getSellingDeniedRecalculationSql()` and executed in `recalculateSellingDenied(?array $productIds)`, where `null` means all products
    - move your own selling denied conditions into an override of `getIndependentSellingDeniedConditionSql()` appended to `parent::getIndependentSellingDeniedConditionSql()` with the aliases `p` (`products`) and `pd` (`product_domains`), and add your parameters to `getIndependentSellingDeniedParameters()` and `getIndependentSellingDeniedParameterTypes()`
    - move your changes of the variant rules into an override of `getSellingDeniedRecalculationSql()`
    - `calculateSellingDeniedForProductIds([])` no longer recalculates all products, call `calculateSellingDeniedForAll()` instead
    - `calculateSellingDeniedForProductIds()` and `recalculateSellingDenied()` now return the IDs of the products whose calculated selling denied changed when product IDs are given, and the statement from `getSellingDeniedRecalculationSql()` has to select them; update your overrides accordingly
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Recalculation\ProductRecalculationFacade::recalculateWithScope()` now receives the recalculation priority as the third argument, add it to your override
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\Flag\PromotionFlagFacade`:
    - `updatePromotionFlags()` was removed, use `updatePromotionFlagsForProducts()` with products loaded by `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\ProductFacade::getAllRefreshedByIdsWithDomainsAndFlagsIndexedById()`
    - the protected method `getUpdatedFlagsOnDomain()` now receives the promotion flag of the product on the domain, computed once per product and domain in `getPromotionFlagsIndexedByProductIdAndDomainId()`; move your changes of the condition when a promotion applies to `findApplicablePromotionXy()`
    - `updatePromotionFlagsForAll()` now clears the entity manager after each batch, so do not call it while you still work with loaded entities
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\GiftPlan\GiftFlagSynchronizerFacade`:
    - `refreshForGiftProductId()` was removed, use `refreshForGiftProductIds()` instead
    - `refreshAllGiftPlans()` now clears the entity manager after each batch, so do not call it while you still work with loaded entities
    - `recalculateGiftFlagForMainProductId()` was removed, use `recalculateGiftFlagForMainProducts()` with products loaded by `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\ProductFacade::getAllRefreshedByIdsWithDomainsAndFlagsIndexedById()` instead
    - the protected method `recalculateGiftFlagForMainProductAndDomainId()` was removed, move your changes to `recalculateGiftFlagForMainProducts()` or `updateGiftFlagOnDomain()`
    - the protected method `shouldHaveGiftFlag()` now receives the gift product IDs of the main product and the IDs of the sellable gift products instead of the main product and the domain ID; move your changes of the gift sellability check to `getSellableGiftProductIdsOnDomain()` and of the flag update to `updateGiftFlagOnDomain()`
- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\GiftPlan\GiftPlanRepository`:
    - `findByGiftProductId()` was removed, use `getMainProductIdsByGiftProductIds()`, which returns the IDs of the main products of the gift plans
    - `findActiveGiftPlansByMainProductAndDomainId()` was removed, use `findActiveGiftProductIdsByMainProductIds()` instead
    - `findAll()` was removed, use `getAllMainProductIds()` to get the IDs of the main products of all gift plans

- `Shopsys\FrameworkBundle\Model\Product\GiftPlan\GiftCartFacade::isGiftProductSellable()` now delegates to `getSellableGiftProductIds()`, which checks the stock and the visibility of all given gift products at once; move your changes of the gift sellability check to `getSellableGiftProductIds()`, or to `getGiftProductsOnStock()` for the stock check
