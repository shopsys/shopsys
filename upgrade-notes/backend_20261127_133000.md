#### Respect Packeta pickup point opening hours in the pickup date ([#5080](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5080))

- the expected delivery date of a carrier's pickup place is now postponed beyond the dates the place is closed on, which the storefront sends in the new `pickupPlaceClosedDates` field of `ChangeTransportInCartInput` and which are stored in the cart
- the following methods have a new optional parameter `array $pickupPlaceClosedDates = []`, so if you override any of them in your project, add the parameter to your implementation:
    - `CartTransportDataFactory::create()`
    - `CartTransportFacade::updateTransportInCart()`
    - `TransportExpectedDeliveryDateCalculation::calculateExpectedDeliveryDateForQuantifiedProducts()`
    - `TransportExpectedDeliveryDateCalculation::calculateDeliveryDate()`
    - `TransportExpectedDeliveryDateCalculation::postponeToFirstAllowedDeliveryDay()`
    - `TransportExpectedDeliveryDateCalculation::isDeliveryAllowedOnDate()`
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
