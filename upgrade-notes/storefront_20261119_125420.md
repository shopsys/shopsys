#### Show cart modifications in a box instead of a toast ([#5051](https://github.com/shopsys/shopsys/pull/5051))

- if your project calls `handleCartModifications()` from `connectors/cart/Cart.ts` or reads `modifications` from `useCurrentCart()`, read the cart modifications from `cartModificationsBatches` in the session store, which `cartModificationsExchange` fills from network responses, and build their texts with `getCartModificationMessages()`
- see #project-base-diff to update your project
