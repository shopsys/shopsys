### Examples
```jsx padded
<SsfwButton name="button1">Default</SsfwButton>
<SsfwButton variant="primary" name="button2">Primary</SsfwButton>
<SsfwButton variant="secondary" name="button3">Secondary</SsfwButton>
```
### Sizes
```jsx padded
<SsfwButton name="button4">Default</SsfwButton>
<SsfwButton size="small" name="button5">Small</SsfwButton>
```

### Combinates of variant and size
```jsx padded
<SsfwButton size="small" variant="secondary">Default</SsfwButton>
<SsfwButton size="small" variant="primary" name="button5">Small</SsfwButton>
```
