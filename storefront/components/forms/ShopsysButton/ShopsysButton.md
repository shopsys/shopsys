### Examples
```jsx padded
<ShopsysButton name="button1">Default</ShopsysButton>
<ShopsysButton variant="primary" name="button2">Primary</ShopsysButton>
<ShopsysButton variant="secondary" name="button3">Secondary</ShopsysButton>
```
### Sizes
```jsx padded
<ShopsysButton name="button4">Default</ShopsysButton>
<ShopsysButton size="small" name="button5">Small</ShopsysButton>
```

### Combinations of variants and sizes
```jsx padded
<ShopsysButton size="small" variant="secondary" name="button6">Default</ShopsysButton>
<ShopsysButton size="small" variant="primary" name="button7">Small</ShopsysButton>
```
